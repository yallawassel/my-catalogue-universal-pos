💳 AI credit requests

Clients request a credit pack and pay you by Whish / OMT / cash. When you receive the money, press Mark paid — the credits are added to their account instantly.

💳 Subscription requests

Shops press Subscribe in their admin. Call them back, agree on the price and payment (cash, Whish, OMT, ZainCash, bank…), then set the plan and a new expiry date in the shop list above and mark the request done. Turn on order alerts in your own shop's admin to get a ring for each request.

🎁 Free trial & subscriptions

New shops start with a free trial with everything on. When the date passes, the catalogue and POS pause automatically (data kept). Setting a new expiry date above switches them back on.
