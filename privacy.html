Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Raffi (myraffi.com) gives shops an online catalogue, ordering and an in-store point of sale (POS). This page explains what information we keep and why.

Who we are

Raffi is operated by Charbel Chidiac, Lebanon. Contact: chidiacch@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Shop owners and staff: name, email address and sign-in details (including your Google account name, email and picture if you sign in with Google), your shop details, products, prices, stock, sales, customers and suppliers you enter.
  • Customers who order from a shop: the name, phone number, delivery address, location (only if you choose to share it) and order details you give when you send an order.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to run and secure the service, and notification tokens if you turn on order alerts.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: show catalogues, pass orders to the shop, run the POS, send alerts the shop owner turned on, prevent abuse, and contact shop owners about their account. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Each shop's data is visible only to that shop's owner and the staff they add. Orders are shared with the shop you order from. We use trusted providers to host the service (Supabase for the database and sign-in, GitHub Pages for the website, Google for sign-in and notifications). If you send an order by WhatsApp or Telegram, those apps' own policies apply.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while the shop's account is active. When a subscription ends, data is kept so the shop can come back. A shop owner can ask us to delete their account and data at any time by email; customers can ask the shop, or us, to delete their order details.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with access rules so one shop cannot read another shop's data.

Children

Raffi is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
