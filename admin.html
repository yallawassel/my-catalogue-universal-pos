Other POS systems

Connector framework

Additional systems can be added through vendor APIs, scheduled exports or their own local connector. No connection is active until its setup and test are completed.

Synchronization safety rules

  • Barcode is the primary product matching key; POS Product ID and SKU are fallbacks.
  • Product names are never used as automatic matching keys.
  • Existing catalogue display names, photos and customer-facing content are preserved when a product matches.
  • Only selected and approved products are sent to the online catalogue.
  • Ambiguous or duplicate identifiers are stopped for manual review.
  • No online system writes back to a connected POS database.
  • Products missing from an import are never automatically deleted.
Connection status

Hybrid Stock Management

Stock is stored in the product's base unit. Additional units convert into that base unit. POS physical stock is the source of truth; online orders create reservations. Reservations are disabled by default until you configure units and opening/POS stock.

Business reservation setting

When enabled, new orders reserve stock only if the order unit has a configured conversion and physical stock has been initialized. Unconfigured or insufficient lines are flagged for review, not silently deducted.

Choose a product to inspect its units and stock.

Product units and barcodes

Every product needs only one base unit. A second or further selling unit is optional and can be added later manually or through the product Excel import. Leave its conversion blank and unverified until you know the exact factor. Example: 1 Box = 12 Rolls means conversion factor 12. Never guess a conversion. Unit-level barcodes are stored here; the current spreadsheet matching screen still matches at product level and will need a later unit-aware import enhancement.

Unit conversion tools — manual + Excel

You can edit a product's units manually above, or export the current unit list to Excel, fill in verified conversion factors, then import it for review. Excel updates existing units only; it never matches by product name and never creates or deletes products or units.

Required columns: Unit ID, Product ID, Conversion to Base, Conversion Configured. Optional: Barcode, Selling Price. For a non-base unit, set Conversion Configured to TRUE only after verifying the exact number of base units it contains. Base units are always kept at factor 1.
