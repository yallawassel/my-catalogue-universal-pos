CATEGORY-LOCAL PRODUCT ORDERING UPDATE

- Product display numbering starts at 1 independently in each category.
- Moving a product reorders only its destination category; moving between categories compacts both source and destination.
- Category order is saved separately in mc_saas_categories.sort_order.
- Public catalogue category cards use the saved category order.
- Use Admin > Category ordering > Renumber products in all categories once to convert existing business-wide numbering to category-local numbering.
- No database schema migration is required. Existing products and images are not deleted.
